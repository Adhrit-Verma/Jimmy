Benchmark

What we measured, and how

Every number here was measured on the development laptop and written down in the project's decision records (D1, D2, …) with the method behind it. Your results will vary with your hardware, your screen and your network.

The machine

A laptop with an AMD Ryzen 7 7840HS, an NVIDIA RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 6 GB of memory (CUDA 12.4), Windows 11 and Python 3.12. It often runs on battery, where Jimmy halves its optional background work.

The agent

130 real commands, frozen from the owner's own sessions, are run through the real pipeline (fast rules first, then the agent), and the first decision is scored. Eight whole tasks run on invented screens that change after each action.

System130 commandsNotes
Before the agent (D41)71 %Rules plus a single tool pick.
Agent (D42)124 / 130 (95 %)Median 0.8 s per decision; two runs gave 124 and 125.
NVIDIA gpt-oss-20b (D43)120 / 130Median 1.4 s per step.
Gemini chain (D58)122 / 130124 picked the right tool; median 1.5 s.

Whole tasks: on 2026-11-01 Gemini first managed 4 of 8 in 143 s and NVIDIA 5 of 8 in 167 s, under the same conditions. Both failed "close this tab" the same way, and the cause was Jimmy, not the model: "yes" pressed a different tab from the one the cursor showed. After that fix and a second one, Gemini did 8 of 8 in 42 s.

Tools per step: 19 core tools, about 1,400 tokens, instead of all 54 at about 3,300 (D48). A two-step screen task takes 2 model calls instead of 4 (D49).

Storage

Screenshots are 1280 px wide so they're readable when opened full size, about 80–88 KB each. That is roughly 30–85 MB per active hour, or 90–250 GB a year at 8 hours a day (D38). The text database is small beside the images.

Weak spots

  • Gemini 3.8 Flash on the free tier timed out or answered "high demand" on 5 of 5 calls when measured; Jimmy now treats slow as down and moves on within 6 seconds.
  • The hosted NVIDIA model sometimes returns an empty answer, once 1 in 5 in a benchmark and in runs on bad days. Jimmy retries, then falls back.
  • RECALL cards are proven only synthetically. The recorded data had no real repeat of an earlier page to find.
  • Hindi accuracy on real speech has not been measured.
  • A "full working day" has not been captured end to end in one run; the longest recorded run was 66 minutes.
  • One machine. None of this has been measured on a second PC yet.

Run them yourself

From the Jimmy folder. The checks need no network; the evaluations call your model provider.

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tests\test_stage1.py      # capture, blur, store
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tests\test_stage3.py      # the card gate
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m ambient replay         # cards over your own history
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tests\eval_agent.py       # 130 real commands (live)
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe tests\eval_trajectory.py  # 8 whole tasks (live)
